How to pay and get access

  1. Select the minutes package you want.
  2. Tap PAY WITH M-PESA.
  3. Wait while the payment screen loads.
  4. After the loading screen, the payment packages will appear again.
  5. Use M-PESA to send the exact amount for your selected package.
  6. After your payment is confirmed, access can be granted.
Make sure you send the exact amount for the package you selected.
