How to pay

  1. Tap PAY WITH M-PESA.
  2. Open your MySafaricom / M-PESA app.
  3. Choose Send Money.
  4. Paste the copied recipient number.
  5. Enter the amount shown for your package.
  6. Confirm the recipient and amount.
  7. Enter your M-PESA PIN.
Your M-PESA PIN is entered only inside the official M-PESA app. Never enter your PIN on this website.
