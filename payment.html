How to pay

1. Select your package.
2. Tap PAY WITH M-PESA.
3. Your recipient number will be copied automatically.
4. My OneApp will open.
5. Open M-PESA → Send Money.
6. Paste the copied number and enter the amount.
7. Confirm and enter your M-PESA PIN.
