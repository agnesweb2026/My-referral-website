How to pay

1. Select your package.
2. Tap PAY WITH M-PESA.
3. The recipient number will be copied.
4. Open your green M-PESA app.
5. Select Send Money.
6. Paste the copied number.
7. Enter the selected amount.
8. Confirm the transaction with your M-PESA PIN.
