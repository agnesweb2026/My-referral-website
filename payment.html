How to pay and get access

  1. Select your minutes package.
  2. Enter the M-PESA number that will make the payment.
  3. Tap PAY WITH M-PESA.
  4. Check your phone for the M-PESA prompt.
  5. Enter your M-PESA PIN on the official M-PESA prompt.
Your M-PESA PIN should only be entered on the official M-PESA prompt.
